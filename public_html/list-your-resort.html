Why KampKiepie

You work hard to keep your resort full. We make sure campers can find it.

Most resorts still rely on word of mouth, a Facebook page, and hoping for the best. Meanwhile, Lekkeslaap and Booking.com are taking 12–15% of every booking and owning the relationship with your guest.

KampKiepie was built specifically for the Southern African camping and outdoor accommodation market — not adapted from some overseas platform. We're proudly local, built in Gauteng, and we understand what matters to a resort owner who has to balance season fluctuations, load shedding, and guests who booked three months ago and forgot to tell you they're bringing a dog.

We're not here to own your guests. We connect them to you, you handle the booking, and you keep the relationship. Our job is to make sure they find you in the first place.

What makes us different
💰 Commission-only. You pay nothing until a guest actually completes a booking. No monthly subscription, no setup fee, no risk to get started.
🎯 Niche audience. Our guests are specifically looking for SA camping and outdoor accommodation — not hotel rooms or city apartments.
🤝 Direct relationship. Guests pay you directly. We don't sit in the middle of the money or harvest guest data.
📱 Online booking system. When you're ready, activate your real-time booking calendar so guests can self-book — no manual back-and-forth required.
🌍 Southern Africa coverage. We list resorts across South Africa and neighbouring countries — Namibia, Botswana, Eswatini, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and more.
The Process

From request to live listing in days

No complicated onboarding. No technical knowledge required. We handle the heavy lifting.

01

Send us your details

WhatsApp, email or contact form — your choice. Tell us your resort name, location and accommodation types. We'll take it from there. No forms to fill, no uploads required from your side.

02

Your free listing goes live

We create a placeholder listing and send you login credentials to manage it. Update your photos, description, facilities and pricing at your own pace — at no cost, with no obligation. Your resort is now discoverable by thousands of campers.

03

Activate live bookings when ready

When you want to take online bookings, we walk you through the simple activation steps — stands, pricing, availability and your booking calendar. Commission only applies when a booking is completed. Until then, everything is free.

Pricing — Simple and Transparent

Everything included. Pay only when guests book and check out.

Your listing and booking system are free to set up. Commission only applies on completed bookings.

📋

Full resort profile

Photos, description, accommodation types, amenities, location map, contact details and more. Everything a guest needs to decide you're the right fit.

📅

Real-time availability calendar

Guests see which stands and units are available for their dates — no more back-and-forth WhatsApps asking "is 15 March open?"

💳

5% on online bookings

When a guest books through the platform, we earn 5% of the booking total — only once the stay is completed. Admin-captured bookings (where you add the booking yourself) are charged at just 2%.

🔔

Instant booking notifications

You get notified the moment a guest submits a booking. Manage everything from your admin panel — confirm, decline, or follow up directly with the guest.

📊

Booking dashboard

See all your bookings, occupancy trends and guest details in one place. Know exactly what's coming up and what needs attention.

🗺️

Map listing

Your resort appears on our interactive map so guests can find accommodation near specific areas, dams or routes — not just by searching your name.

A minimum monthly fee of R99 applies when online bookings are active — only payable if your monthly commission falls below this amount. No surprises, no hidden costs.
