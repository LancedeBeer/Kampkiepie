KampKiepie 04

How it works

Simple from start to settled.

No complexity. No surprises. This is the entire cycle.

1
Guest books
A traveller finds your resort on kampkiepie.co.za and makes a booking. You get their details immediately.
2
You confirm
You manage the booking through your listing. Payment goes directly from the guest to you — we never touch it.
3
Guest checks out
Only at checkout does commission become calculable. Not at booking. Not before. Only when the stay is complete.
4
You report & pay
Submit your monthly booking report by the 7th. Pay commission by the 15th. That is the whole relationship.

Pioneer rates

Two rates. Both triggered at checkout only.

KampKiepie booking
2.5%
A guest finds you on the platform and books independently. 2.5% of their booking value, payable after they check out.
Admin booking — your guest
1.0%
A guest calls you, walks in, or messages you directly. You capture the booking in the system yourself. Because you found them, you pay 1% — not 2.5%.
Listing fee
R0
No monthly subscription. No setup fee. No minimum charge. Nothing until a real guest has checked out of your property.
Locked permanently. Your Pioneer rates are fixed for as long as your agreement is in force. When KampKiepie grows and standard rates increase, your rates stay exactly where they are today.
