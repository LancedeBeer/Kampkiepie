How can we help?

Answers to common questions about finding, viewing, and booking a resort on KampKiepie.

🔍
🔍 Finding a Resort 📄 Viewing a Resort 📅 Booking
🔍
Finding a Resort
Covers the homepage and full resort directory.
How do I search for a resort?▾
Use the search bar on the homepage, or the search box on the resort directory page, to search by resort name, city, or province. On the directory page you can also filter by province using the area dropdown.
What do the category icons mean?▾
Each icon represents a type of accommodation or a feature a resort offers — like Camp, Glamp, Chalet, B&B, or Online Booking. On the homepage, click "View all categories" to see the full list, then the green "What do these mean?" button for a plain-language explanation of each one.
What do the badges on resort cards mean?▾
Small badges on resort cards give you a quick read at a glance: 🔒 members-only access, 📅 online booking available, 🐾 pet-friendly, plus badges for Niche stays, Weddings, Conferences, Functions, and Hunting where a resort offers them.
Can I filter by area or province?▾
Yes — both the homepage search and the full directory have an "All Areas" dropdown next to the search bar, letting you narrow results down to a specific province.
Is KampKiepie free to use?▾
Yes — browsing, searching, and viewing resort listings is completely free for guests.
📄
Viewing a Resort
Covers individual resort profile pages.
How do I contact a resort directly?▾
Every resort page has a Contact section listing the ways you can reach that specific resort — usually WhatsApp, email, and/or phone. Click any of them to get in touch directly.
How do I know if I can book online?▾
If a resort supports online booking, you'll see a "Book Now" button near the top of the page (just under the address) and again further down. If there's no Book Now button, you'll need to contact the resort directly to check availability and arrange your stay.
Why does "Book Now" sometimes take me to a different-looking site?▾
Some resorts use their own booking system or a third-party platform (like NightsBridge or Q2B) instead of KampKiepie's own booking page. In that case, "Book Now" takes you straight to that resort's own system.
Is my information tracked when I view a resort page?▾
We count anonymous page views so resorts can see how much interest their listing is getting, but this doesn't identify you personally. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
📅
Booking
Covers the KampKiepie booking page, for resorts that support online booking.
How does the booking process work?▾
Choose your check-in and check-out dates, then browse the stands or units that are actually available for those dates. Select the one you want, fill in your details, and submit your booking request directly to the resort.
Can I choose a specific stand or unit?▾
Yes — the booking page shows you the stands or units that are genuinely free for your selected dates, so you can pick the one that suits you rather than being assigned one automatically.
What if my dates aren't available?▾
The booking page only shows units that are actually free for the dates you've chosen. If nothing matches, try adjusting your dates, or contact the resort directly to ask about a waitlist or alternative options.
Do I need to pay a deposit?▾
Deposit requirements are set individually by each resort. Where applicable, the resort will follow up on your submitted booking with deposit details and payment instructions.
I submitted a booking — what happens next?▾
You'll get a booking reference once you submit. The resort itself processes your request from there — since exact next steps vary by resort, keep an eye on your contact details for a follow-up, or reach out to the resort directly if you're unsure.
