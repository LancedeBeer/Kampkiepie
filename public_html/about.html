About KampKiepie

Helping South Africans find their perfect getaway

We built KampKiepie because finding a good campsite, cabin, or getaway shouldn't mean scrolling through ten different websites and Facebook groups. One place, every kind of stay, from POP stopovers to full family lodges.

Our Story

Built by people who actually camp.

KampKiepie began life in July 2025 as an app — a simple idea to bring South Africa's incredible campsites, guesthouses, and getaways together in one place, instead of scattered across ten different websites and Facebook groups.

As the vision for KampKiepie grew, it became clear the original platform simply couldn't keep up — it was too restrictive and too slow to support where we wanted to take things next. So we made the call to rebuild KampKiepie from the ground up as a fully optimised website, built to handle the scale and features our vision actually needs.

That rebuild is what powers KampKiepie today: a directory and booking platform that understands how South Africans really travel — WhatsApp-first, community-driven, and covering everything from a weekend POP stopover to a week-long family lodge booking.

"KampKiepie is a family-owned business at heart. We didn't want another booking site that only understands hotels — campers, caravaners, and long-stayers deserve a platform built around how they actually travel, which is exactly why we rebuilt KampKiepie the right way instead of settling for a platform that couldn't grow with us."
— The de Beer Family, KampKiepie

The People Behind It
Meet the team
🧑‍💻
David de Beer
Founder & Developer
David studied programming at NWU in the nineties, spent a few years coding, and then life happened. He picked it back up in 2024 — and KampKiepie is what came out of it. The idea was simple: small resorts deserve a fair deal, and paying 15% commission to platforms that own your guests is not a fair deal. His day job is in cellular sales, so he knows exactly what it feels like to be on the receiving end of a pitch that sounds too good to be true.
sales@kampkiepie.co.za
