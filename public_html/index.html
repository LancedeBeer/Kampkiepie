What do these categories mean?

KampKiepie is Southern Africa's directory for campsites, caravan parks, glamping spots, chalets and lodges — built by a family that actually camps, not adapted from some overseas platform. Whether you're chasing a dam-side stand for the weekend, a self-catering chalet in the Drakensberg, or a proper bushveld escape in Limpopo, our directory covers all nine provinces with 750+ resorts and counting.

No middleman standing between you and the resort. Search by name, area or accommodation type, check real availability, and book directly — your money and your details go straight to the people running the place, not through us. That's how it should work.

Own or manage a resort? List it on KampKiepie — commission-only, no monthly fees.
