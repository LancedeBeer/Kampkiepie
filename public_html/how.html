How It Works

Finding and booking a getaway, simplified

Whether you're after a weekend POP stopover or a week-long family lodge booking, here's exactly how KampKiepie gets you there.

For Guests
Four simple steps
No account needed to browse — just search, compare, and get in touch or book directly.
1
🔍
Search or browse
Use the homepage search bar, filter by category (Camp, Chalet, Glamp, and more), or browse the full directory by area, name, or activity.
2
📄
Check the details
Every resort has its own page: photos, amenities, accommodation types, pricing hints, and direct contact details for the host.
3
📅
Book or enquire
Resorts with online booking let you pick your stand or unit and dates right there. Others, you'll contact directly via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
4
🎒
Pack your bags
Once confirmed, you're set. Keep your booking reference handy, and reach out to the host directly if your plans change.
Two Ways to Book
Not every resort books the same way
KampKiepie is a directory first — some resorts book instantly through us, others use their own systems. We're upfront about which is which on every resort page.
📅 Online Booking (KampKiepie)
Look for the "Online Booking" badge and a "Book Now" button. You'll pick your stand, dates, and guest count, and submit your booking directly — no back-and-forth needed.
💬 Direct Contact
Some resorts prefer WhatsApp, email, or a phone call, or use their own external booking platform. Their resort page will always show the fastest way to reach them.
For Resort Owners
Want to list your resort?
Getting listed on KampKiepie puts your resort in front of travellers actively searching for a place like yours. Reach out via the button below, or through the "List your resort" link at the bottom of any page.
Still Have Questions?
We've got a Help Centre for that
Common questions about searching, booking, and using the site are answered in our Help Centre.
